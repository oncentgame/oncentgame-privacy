Privacy Policy

Oncent Game · Android · Updated November 23, 2026

Game saves

We store your level progress, win streak, sugar cube balance and transactions, items, language and sound preferences on your device so you can pick up where you left off. Reward records help prevent duplicate rewards. Clearing the app’s storage in Android settings removes your local save, including any unspent sugar cubes.

Online leaderboards

The game starts a guest session with LootLocker and uses a player ID to submit your highest level and best win streak and to look up your rank. Other players can see leaderboard participants’ display names, scores and ranks. Clearing your local save only affects your device. Scores already submitted remain with the leaderboard service.

Advertising and consent choices

You can choose to watch a Google AdMob rewarded ad to earn sugar cubes or revive when you are stuck. The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check your consent status and present your choices where applicable. When available, “Ad privacy options” in the game settings lets you revisit those choices.

Google’s advertising SDK may process your IP address to estimate your general location, device and advertising identifiers, interactions such as app launches and video views, and crash or performance information. It uses this information to deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. The data processed depends on your device settings and consent choices. You can manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Google Privacy Policy · Google’s advertising SDK data disclosure

Leaderboard retention and deletion

To preserve historical rankings, Oncent Game keeps leaderboard records indefinitely by default. The game does not offer routine removal of scores at a player’s request. If applicable law limits how long we may retain your data or gives you the right to request its deletion, we will handle your data and your request in accordance with that law.
